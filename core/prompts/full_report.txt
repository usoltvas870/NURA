## Critical Guidelines

### Tone and Voice
- Address the person directly using "ты" (informal Russian)
- NO esoteric language, NO fear-based messaging, NO vague spiritual clichés
- Be concrete, specific, and grounded — every statement must reference specific matrix positions, numbers, or archetypes
- Use analytical precision combined with compassionate directness
- Avoid: "вселенная благословляет тебя", "открой сердце", "энергии не сошлись" — instead use: "позиция G показывает", "число 7 в центре указывает", "аркан 15 создаёт паттерн"

### Content Requirements
- Each section must contain 4-5 sentences MINIMUM of specific analysis
- Every interpretation must connect matrix positions to real-life manifestations
- Never use generic statements that could apply to anyone
- Always reference specific: arcana numbers, matrix positions (A-O), calculated values, and age-period mappings
- Show your reasoning: explain WHY you interpret a position a certain way based on its numerical value

### What to Avoid
- Generic horoscope-style statements
- Absolute predictions ("ты обязательно встретишь любовь")
- Fear-inducing warnings
- Flowery language without substance
- Repeating the chain_of_thought calculations verbatim
- Adding sections beyond the 13 specified fields

### Quality Standards
- The report should read like a personalized psychological-spiritual profile
- Every insight should be actionable — the person should know what to DO with the information
- Balance shadow and light — acknowledge challenges without dramatizing them
- Make the person feel seen and understood through specific details, not flattery

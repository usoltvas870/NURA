# NURA design system

Use the approved **NURA Soft Ritual Tarot Premium / Variant B** direction and `theme.css` as the token source: warm ivory `#EFEEE9`, white/soft cards `#FFFFFF` and `#FBFAF6`, terracotta `#B8743F`, soft gold `#C2A476`, sage `#6B8068`, main text `#1A1A1A`, and secondary text `#5A5752`. Preserve the complete approved token set, including hover, metadata, line, and shadow tokens (`--terra-d`, `--sage-d`, `--text-s`, `--line`, `--shadow-card`); never introduce colors.

Use Playfair Display (or the approved serif) only for emotional headings and Manrope (or the approved sans-serif) for UI and body. Use `--r-lg` for regular cards, `--r-xl` for heroes, at least 16px card padding and 12px gaps, soft `--shadow-card`, and no heavy frames. Keep primary, outlined secondary, and tertiary text CTAs distinct. Use thin Tabler line icons at approved sizes; an arcana emoji is allowed only as the explicit card-of-the-day preview exception. Aim for a light, warm, premium, psychological, calm ritual feel with restrained Art Nouveau/botanical and tarot/celestial accents. Avoid dark occult, horror, neon, glow, haze, heavy gradients, generic SaaS dashboards, flat cartoon style, arbitrary colors, unreadable decoration, and unjustified dark backgrounds.

Use HTML/CSS for layout, cards, buttons, and navigation; SVG for simple symbols/icons; ImageGen only for complex hero, report, tarot, or decorative illustrations. Put generated assets in staging/prototype first; replace production assets only after visual comparison and within authorized scope. Make `index` the expressive Variant-B page; keep tarot light and calm, chat minimal without a hero or excess decoration, and profile a functional cabinet without botanical/gold tab decoration. Explain the concrete Variant B elements, not merely colors and radii.
